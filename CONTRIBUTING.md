# Contributing to Vanylith

Thank you for considering a contribution to Vanylith.

## Required contributor agreement

Every external Contribution proposed for inclusion in Vanylith must be made
under the [Vanylith Contributor License Agreement 1.0](CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md)
(VCLA 1.0). The Contributor must affirmatively accept VCLA 1.0 in the pull
request or through another written contribution process designated by the
Project Steward. Acceptance of VCLA 1.0 is not acceptance of a future version.

Public Project Steward identity: `@ByNovaLin`

Official repository identifier: `https://github.com/ByNovaLin/vanylith`

Under VCLA 1.0, the Project Steward is the individual or legal entity that
lawfully operates that public GitHub account and administers the official
Vanylith repository under that public project identity. The identity is not an
abstract brand or community and is not represented as a separate corporation.

The Contributor retains copyright. VCLA 1.0 does not assign copyright,
but it gives the Project Steward the perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
irrevocable, royalty-free, fully paid-up sublicensing and relicensing rights
needed to publish the Contribution under the Vanylith Community Source License
1.0 and to include it in separate commercial, OEM, hosted-service, proprietary,
or other written licenses. VCLA 1.0 also provides a narrowly scoped,
sublicensable patent license covering only the Contribution Patent Claims
defined in that Agreement.

Maintainers must not merge an external Contribution unless the Contributor has
affirmatively accepted VCLA 1.0. A pull-request checkbox or equivalent written
statement records acceptance in the project workflow; this document does not
claim that any particular electronic method is necessarily conclusive in every
jurisdiction.

The Contributor does not need the Project Steward's real name, government-issued
identifier, home address, private contact information, or other private identity
information in order to contribute. No government-issued identifier or other
unnecessary personal information is required from the Contributor.

## Rights and third-party material

By submitting, the Contributor confirms that the Contributor owns the
Contribution or has sufficient authority to provide it under VCLA 1.0. If
employer or client authorization is required, the Contributor must obtain it
before submission.

If a Contribution incorporates third-party material, identify it clearly and
include its source, exact version where applicable, license, copyright notices,
attribution requirements, and source and binary redistribution conditions in
the pull request.

## Practical requirements

- Keep changes focused and explain their purpose.
- Do not remove `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, VCLA 1.0, or
  applicable third-party notices.
- Do not include secrets, private keys, generated wallet files, or credentials.
- Follow the existing build and test instructions in `README.md`.

"Contribution" has the meaning given in VCLA 1.0.
