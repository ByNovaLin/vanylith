# Vanylith Contributor License Agreement 1.0

The Vanylith Contributor License Agreement 1.0 ("VCLA 1.0") is a lightweight
contributor agreement for contributions to Vanylith. It does not transfer
ownership of a Contribution.

## 1. Definitions and acceptance

"Agreement" means this VCLA 1.0.

"Project Steward" means the individual or legal entity that, when VCLA 1.0
first becomes effective for Contributions, lawfully operates the GitHub account
publicly identified as @ByNovaLin and, under that public project identity,
administers the official Vanylith repository identified as
https://github.com/ByNovaLin/vanylith. Thereafter, another individual or legal
entity becomes the Project Steward only through a permitted assignment to a
Successor Steward under Section 7. Mere control, sale, transfer, compromise,
loss, username reassignment, or repository technical-control change does not by
itself confer Project Steward status. The Project Steward accepts the rights
granted by this Agreement. The account name is a public project identifier; it
does not create or represent a separate legal entity, and this Agreement does
not require publication of the Project Steward's private legal name.

"Successor Steward" means an individual or legal entity that in good faith
assumes primary stewardship of Vanylith and either (a) succeeds the Project
Steward in connection with a genuine transfer of responsibility for the
project, or (b) acquires all or substantially all of the Vanylith-related
copyrights, project assets, or business and continues to administer Vanylith.
It does not include a party that acquires only this Agreement or its Contributor
grants without succeeding to the Vanylith project.

"Contributor" means the individual copyright owner, or the legal entity or
authorized representative of a copyright owner, that intentionally submits a
Contribution and affirmatively accepts this Agreement.

"Contribution" means source code, documentation, tests, assets, or other
copyrightable material intentionally submitted for inclusion in Vanylith. It
does not include a bug report, feature request, general discussion, or material
conspicuously marked in writing as "Not a Contribution" before submission.

This Agreement applies when a Contributor intentionally submits a Contribution
for inclusion in Vanylith and affirmatively states acceptance of the Vanylith
Contributor License Agreement 1.0 (VCLA 1.0), including through the project
pull-request process or another written submission process designated by the
Project Steward.

## 2. Contributor retains ownership

The Contributor retains copyright in the original content of the Contribution
that the Contributor owns and may continue to use, license, or otherwise
exploit that content independently. This Agreement is not a copyright
assignment and does not transfer ownership of the Contribution to the Project
Steward or any other person.

The Contributor's independent rights remain subject to rights in pre-existing
Vanylith material, third-party components, and other material the Contributor
does not own. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Contributor additional
rights in pre-existing Vanylith or third-party material incorporated into,
modified by, or necessary to exercise the Contribution. In particular, this
Agreement does not authorize the Contributor to relicense pre-existing
Vanylith material under different terms.

## 5. Authority and third-party material

The Contributor represents that the Contributor owns the Contribution or has
sufficient authority to submit it and grant the rights in Sections 3 and 4. If
the Contribution was created in the course of employment or for another person
or entity, the Contributor represents that any authorization required from that
person or entity has been obtained.

The Contributor must clearly identify all third-party material included in a
Contribution and provide its source, applicable license, copyright notices, and
other required notices. This Agreement does not grant rights in identified
third-party material beyond the rights the Contributor is authorized to grant.
The Contributor represents that, to the Contributor's knowledge, the submission
does not intentionally violate a third party's rights.

## 6. No unnecessary identity requirement

Acceptance of this Agreement does not require a government-issued identifier or
other unnecessary personal information. A contribution workflow may retain the
account name, commit metadata, affirmative acceptance record, and other
information reasonably needed to administer the contribution and document this
Agreement.

A Contributor does not need to know, collect, or obtain the Project Steward's
private legal name, government-issued identifier, home address, private contact
information, or other private identity information in order to submit a
Contribution or accept this Agreement.

## 7. Limited assignment to a Successor Steward

The Project Steward may assign this Agreement and the rights it receives under
this Agreement only to a Successor Steward and only as part of a genuine
succession to Vanylith project stewardship or a transaction involving all or
substantially all of the relevant Vanylith copyrights, project assets, or
business. The Successor Steward must assume the Project Steward's applicable
obligations under this Agreement and then becomes the Project Steward for
purposes of this Agreement.

The Project Steward may not sell, assign, or transfer this Agreement or its
Contributor grants as standalone assets to an unrelated party that does not
qualify as a Successor Steward. Any other assignment requires the Contributor's
written consent. A permitted assignment does not expand the scope of the rights
originally granted by the Contributor.

Mere control, transfer, sale, compromise, loss, or reassignment of the
@ByNovaLin GitHub account, its username, its credentials, or the official
Vanylith repository does not by itself make another person the Project Steward
or assign this Agreement or any Contributor grant. A change in technical control
has legal effect under this Agreement only if it is accompanied by a permitted
assignment to a Successor Steward under this Section.

## 8. No implied transfer or waiver

Except for the express licenses in Sections 3 and 4, each party retains its
rights. No copyright or patent ownership is transferred, and no additional
rights are granted by implication, estoppel, or otherwise. A waiver of this
Agreement must be explicit
and in writing.

version. The English text is authoritative; any translation is for convenience

A change to this Agreement must be published as a new version. Acceptance of
VCLA 1.0 does not constitute acceptance of any future version, including a
future VCLA 2.0. A Contributor must separately and affirmatively accept a future
version before that future version applies to a Contribution from that
Contributor. Rights already granted under VCLA 1.0 remain governed by VCLA 1.0
unless the Contributor separately agrees otherwise in writing.
